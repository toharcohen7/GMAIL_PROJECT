# Email Management

This guide covers the email management features in the Gmail Project.

## Inbox Management

### Viewing Your Emails

The inbox interface is organized into several key areas:

- **Sidebar**: Access system and custom labels
- **Email List**: View emails in the currently selected label
- **Email Preview**: Read the selected email content

To view emails:
1. Select a label from the sidebar (Inbox, Sent, Draft, etc.)
2. The corresponding emails will appear in the email list
3. Click on an email to view its contents in the preview pane

### System Labels

The Gmail Project includes the following system labels:

- **Inbox**: New emails you've received
- **Sent**: Emails you've sent to others
- **Draft**: Emails you've started but haven't sent
- **Spam**: Emails identified as potentially unwanted
- **Trash**: Emails you've deleted

## Email Actions

### Composing New Emails

1. Click the "Compose" button in the top-left corner
2. In the compose form that appears:
   - Enter recipient usernames in the "To" field (separated by commas)
   - Enter a subject in the "Subject" field
   - Write your message in the main text area
3. Click "Send" to send immediately

### Managing Drafts

1. If you close the compose form before sending, your email is saved as a draft
2. Access your drafts by clicking on the "Draft" label in the sidebar
3. Click on a draft to continue editing
4. When ready, click "Send" to send the email

### Marking Emails as Read/Unread

1. Select one or more emails by checking the boxes next to them
2. Click the "Mark as Read" (envelope open icon) or "Mark as Unread" (envelope icon) button

### Starring Important Emails

1. Click the star icon next to an email to mark it as important
2. Click the star again to remove the star
3. View all starred emails by selecting the "Starred" label

### Deleting Emails

1. Select one or more emails
2. Click the "Delete" (trash icon) button
3. The email will be moved to the Trash label
4. To permanently delete:
   - Go to the Trash label
   - Select the emails you want to permanently delete
   - Click the "Delete" button again

### Moving Emails Between Labels

1. Select one or more emails
2. Click the "Move" (folder icon) button
3. Select the destination label
4. The emails will be moved to that label

## Search Functionality

The search bar at the top of the inbox allows you to find emails quickly:

1. Click in the search bar
2. Type your search terms
3. Press Enter or click the search button
4. Results matching your search will be displayed

The search looks for matches in:
- Sender name
- Subject line
- Email content

## Email Organization

### Creating Custom Labels

1. In the sidebar, locate the "Labels" section
2. Click the "+" icon
3. Enter a name for your new label
4. Click "Create"

### Applying Labels to Emails

1. Select one or more emails
2. Click the "Move" button
3. Select the label you want to apply
4. The emails will be moved to that label

### Managing Labels

1. Hover over a label in the sidebar
2. Click the menu icon (three dots)
3. Choose from:
   - Edit: Change the label name
   - Delete: Remove the label

## Email Pagination

Navigate through your emails:

1. Use the pagination controls at the top of the email list
2. Click Previous/Next to move between pages
3. The system displays a limited number of emails per page for better performance
