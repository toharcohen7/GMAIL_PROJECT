# Labels and Organization

This guide explains how to use labels and organization features in the Gmail Project to manage your emails effectively.

## Understanding Labels

In the Gmail Project, labels function as organizational categories for your emails:

- Each email can only have one label at a time
- System labels like "Inbox," "Sent," and "Trash" are provided by default
- You can create your own custom labels for better organization

## System Labels

The Gmail Project includes the following built-in labels:

- **Inbox (Received)**: Default location for new emails you've received
- **Sent**: Contains emails you've sent to others
- **Draft**: Stores emails you've started but haven't sent yet
- **Spam**: Contains emails identified as potential spam
- **Trash**: Holds emails you've deleted
- **Starred**: Special view showing emails you've marked with a star

## Creating and Managing Custom Labels

### Creating New Labels

1. In the sidebar, find the "Labels" section
2. Click the "+" button next to "Labels"
3. In the modal that appears, enter a name for your new label
4. Click "Save" to create the label

### Editing Labels

1. In the sidebar, hover over the custom label you want to edit
2. Click the three dots (⋮) that appear
3. Select "Edit" from the dropdown menu
4. Enter a new name for your label
5. Click "Save" to update the label

### Deleting Labels

1. In the sidebar, hover over the custom label you want to delete
2. Click the three dots (⋮) that appear
3. Select "Delete" from the dropdown menu
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

**Note:** Deleting a label doesn't delete the emails. Emails with deleted labels will be moved to their original system labels based on their status (Sent or Received).

## Moving Emails Between Labels

### From the Email List View

1. Select one or more emails by checking the boxes next to them
2. Click the "Move to label" button (tag icon) in the toolbar
3. Select the destination label from the dropdown
4. The emails will be moved to that label

### Viewing Emails by Label

1. Click on any label in the sidebar
2. The email list will update to show only emails with that label
3. The unread count next to each label shows how many unread emails it contains

## Label Management Best Practices

1. **Use meaningful names** for your labels to easily identify their purpose
2. **Keep your label list manageable** by not creating too many labels
3. **Regularly clean up** by deleting unused labels
4. **Check your spam folder** periodically for legitimate emails
5. **Use stars** to mark important emails across different labels

## Limitations

- An email can only have one label at a time
- When moving an email to a new label, it's removed from its previous label
- System labels (Sent, Draft, Received, Spam, Trash) cannot be edited or deleted

## Search and Filtering

The search function at the top of the page allows you to find emails across all your labels:

1. Enter your search term in the search box
2. Results will show matching emails from all labels
3. Click on a specific label in the sidebar to narrow results to that label
